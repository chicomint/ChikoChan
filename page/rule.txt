# Rules

-Please make sure all posts follow the site rules and applicable U.S. laws.
-Be respectful and kind to others.
-No NSFW content.
-Do not harass, threaten, or bully other users.
-Do not post your own or someone else's private or personal information.
-No spam or excessive repeated posts.

# Privacy Policy

-We do not store your IP address.
-Site data includes posts and timestamps, moderation records, staff accounts, and settings.
-Browser preferences (theme, hidden posts, and saved deletion passwords) are encrypted in the database and associated with a random private cookie. Clearing that cookie starts fresh preferences.
-Abuse controls use keyed fingerprints rather than storing raw IP addresses. Optional external verification providers process requests when enabled.
