# About

https://github.com/chicomint/ChikoChan

You can post whatever is on your mind — pictures, random thoughts, memes, art, games, hobbies, and more!

Some boards have specific topics or categories, so please make sure your posts belong on that board and follow the rules:

https://boards.chiko.cc/rules

## History
ChikoChan was started by me on **June 20, 2026**, inspired by imageboards such as 4chan and others.
At first, ChikoChan didn't even have a homepage. It only had **/chiko/**, which was the very first board.
A few weeks after opening, the site started receiving a lot of spam posts. You can read more about what happened here:

**https://chiko.cc/all_blog#:~:text=someone%20try,protection**

And yeah... that's pretty much the story of ChikoChan so far.

# FAQ

### What is an imageboard?
An imageboard is a type of online forum where people can create threads, post images, and reply to other posts. Don't need an account.

### What is a fortune?
A fortune is a small random message or result that can appear in a post when using the fortune feature.

### What is sage?
Sage lets you reply to a thread without bumping it back to the top of the board.

### Can I delete posts I make?
Yes! You can delete your own posts by selecting the checkbox next to the post, then clicking the Remove button at the bottom-right of the page.
If you set a password when making the post, enter that password to delete it. If you didn't set one, ChikoChan usually creates a password automatically, so you can normally just click Delete. ^-^

### What is the pink text?
Pink text is ChikoChan's version of **greentext**, a style commonly used on imageboards such as 4chan.
Greentext is text that appears green when a line starts with `>`. People often use it for story, jokes, or describing something that happened.
On ChikoChan, it works the same way, except the text appears **pink** instead of green.
